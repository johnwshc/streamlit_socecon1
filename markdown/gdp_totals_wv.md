## GDP Totals 2017 - 2024

Key Moments in the North Dakota GDP Timeline

-  **2008**: The state's GDP reflects a *Non-Event* in response to the 
great Recession, reflecting the extraordinary resilience of the 'socialised' state bank.
- **2012**: Big surge in oil production, leading to a substantial increase in GDP.
- **2017**: The state's GDP reached $100 billion, marking a significant milestone in its economic growth.
- **2020**: The COVID-19 pandemic and Russia oil sanctions.
- **2024**: North Dakota's GDP is projected to reach $150 billion, reflecting continued economic resilience and growth.
